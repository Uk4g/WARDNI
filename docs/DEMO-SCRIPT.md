# Demo video script — 3 minutes, screen recording + voiceover, no face

Record the screen only. Talk over it in English. Keep the SIMULATED label on screen.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem
> "One nurse, a whole ward at night. She walks room to room to check, to answer,
> to remember — and at 5am she rewrites the whole night by hand for 30 to 60 minutes.
> This dashboard cuts the walking, the waiting, and the writing."

Show: Home page, problem list.

## 0:20–1:40 — Live scripted night
Press Play scripted night. Narrate each event as it appears, in order:
- "Room 203, turn due — 2 hours on the back, time to turn."
- "Room 201, pre-fall — sat up, legs over the rail. Check before standing. Wireframe only, no raw video."
- "Room 205, voice: I'm in pain. Spark says RED — pain plus post-op, check first."
- "Room 202, blanket — YELLOW, comfort, not urgent."
- "Room 204, breakfast — auto-answered, 08:00, no walk needed."
- "Room 206, IV line tugged — RED, vision flagged it."
- Click Acknowledge, click Mark turn done.

## 1:40–2:30 — The Meta models
Show code briefly, one screen per model, no API key on screen:
- "SAM 3.1 — vision, wireframe, bed-turn, bed-exit, line tug."
- "Muse Voice Transcribe — intercom, transcript, intent."
- "Muse Spark 1.3 — priority plus WHY, on the tile, not just a colour."
- "Three models, one tile, one action. That's the agentic flow."

## 2:30–3:00 — Handoff + close
Press Generate morning handoff.
> "The whole night, in seconds, from the event log. Not a clinical device,
> not medical advice — simulated demo, fictional patients, nurse in the loop.
> One nurse. One dashboard. A whole ward at night."

End screen: Live demo link + repo link + SIMULATED label. Stop at 3:00.
